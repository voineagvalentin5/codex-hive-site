05 / Founder & lead researcher

Valentin Voineag.

Valentin Voineag is an independent researcher, appliance technician, and minister based in Michigan. His background is in practical diagnostics: taking complex systems apart to understand why they fail. He applies that same logic to theoretical physics: strip away the noise, find the pattern, test it.

That approach drives the Fractal Time Framework, which he is testing across systems from earthquake sequences and neural oscillations to galactic rotation curves. Codex Hive Labs is where that work lives.
