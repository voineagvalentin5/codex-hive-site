02 / Codex Hive Interactive

Two games in the works.

Skyborne comes first. Synapse is next, built for VR headsets.

G01In production

Skyborne

Our first game is still in production, and we're expecting an early alpha build by early December.

G02In development

Synapse

Two Minds. One Machine.

Our next game is in development in Unity for Meta Quest and other VR headsets. You step into the cockpit of a giant mech as the pilot, with an AI co-pilot at your side. Together you sync up, read the battlefield, and move as one. We're aiming to release it in mid-2027.

03 / Featured app

Interlinear Bible study

Hive Scribe.

A free interlinear Bible study app. Read the whole Bible with Hebrew, Greek (LXX), Aramaic Targum and Latin Vulgate side by side, explore word details, cross-reference chords, and guided studies. Works on phone and offline.
