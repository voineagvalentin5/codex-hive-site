The World Gets Big

From Codex Hive InteractivePublished October 20, 2026

What if the city was only the beginning?

In the Sep 28 build, Skyborne left the skyline behind. We added biomes, so now you can cross a forested Amazon, cruise over open Sahara sand and climb above the clouds, with a city shrinking beneath you. A storm build added dark skies over the towers too.

They're simple shapes for now: low trees, flat dunes, rough clouds. But for the first time it feels like flying over a planet instead of a level. Next up is making each place feel alive.

Patrons see these builds first. Come fly with us.
