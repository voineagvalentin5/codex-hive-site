A correction from the Hive

November 5, 2026

In the Hive, the possibilities are endless. We turn what we gather into honey, including the hard parts.

This week Codex Hive Research posted a public correction on codex-hive.com.

Earlier papers and Zenodo records of mine said a log-periodic correction with λ ≈ e explained SPARC galaxy rotation curves without dark matter. I'm withdrawing those claims. Two new tests, with methods locked in advance, found no such effect, and any ripple of that kind is below about 2%. My earlier work does not show that dark matter is unnecessary.

The full notice is live and linked from the Research menu, and corrected Zenodo versions are up. If you'd like the longer null-result write-up, just ask.

Honest work means saying what held and what didn't. That's the standard I'm holding myself to.

Valentin Voineag, Codex Hive Labs
