A new home for Codex Hive, and a first look at Hive Academy

From Codex Hive LabsPublished November 19, 2026

Codex Hive has a new foundation. This week codex-hive.com moved to a faster, simpler home, and every Hive app now shares one navigation bar and one logo. You can move from Hive Buzz Newz to Hive Astra to Hive Scribe without feeling like you've left the Hive.

Hive Academy: now in preview

Our education division, Hive Academy, is open as an early preview at academy.codex-hive.com. Its first featured experience is Hive Astra, an interactive solar system and live space science workbench. More lessons and tools will join it as they're ready.

What's new in the apps

  • Hive Astra v3: a redesign with Live Signals and Missions. astra.codex-hive.com
  • Hive-Babble: a new download page. babble.codex-hive.com
  • Hive Scribe: a Bible study tool. Read a verse, explore its words, follow the connections. scribe.codex-hive.com
  • Hive Buzz Newz: daily news from the Hive. newz.codex-hive.com

Music and podcasts

Hive Buzz Records and ValenVox stay all about the music. Our podcasts are getting their own name: Hive Talks. From the Hive.

Games

Codex Hive Interactive is still building Skyborne, with an early alpha aimed at early December. Synapse, our VR mech game ("Two Minds. One Machine."), comes next.

Thanks for exploring with us.
