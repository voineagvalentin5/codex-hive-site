Join the Hive

From Codex Hive InteractivePublished October 29, 2026

Codex Hive Labs is one small team with a few big passions.

There's Skyborne, the flying game you've watched grow over the past two weeks, from Codex Hive Interactive. There's Hive Buzz Records, our music label, home to TheArtificialPulse and Asher Skye. There's Codex Hive Research, and Codex Hive Publishing is on the way. All of it is made in the Hive.

Patreon is where it all comes together. Members get build updates, behind-the-scenes looks and new music as it's made, and every membership helps us keep building.

If you've enjoyed this series, we'd love to have you with us. Pick a tier on Patreon and say hi. You'll find more at codex-hive.com.
