Meet the divisions of Codex Hive Labs

From Codex Hive LabsPublished October 30, 2026

In the Hive, the possibilities are endless. We turn what we gather into honey.

This week every part of Codex Hive Labs got its own name and crest:

- Hive Buzz Records. From the Hive. Our music label, home to new artists and original releases. It started at home: I wanted my kids to have clean music in the styles we love, made with intentions I believe in.

- Codex Hive Interactive. Worlds Worth Entering. Original games from the Hive, starting with Skyborne.

- Codex Hive Research. Turning patterns into tools: original research and technology, from Fractal Time to CombOS.

- Hive Academy (coming soon). Learning from the Hive: hands-on education in science, AI technology and creativity.

- Codex Hive Publishing (coming soon). Stories from the Hive: original books, worlds and research, written and published in-house.

You choose what's next. Starting Nov 1, we're running a monthly Hive Vote on https://www.patreon.com/c/Codex_Hive_Labs. It's free to vote, and the winning project gets the month's main focus. The first vote is open Nov 1 to Nov 7.

Patrons also get behind-the-scenes looks, like this week's first look at the self-healing swarm design behind CombOS and new Skyborne sneak peeks starting Nov 1.

Valentin Voineag, Codex Hive Labs
