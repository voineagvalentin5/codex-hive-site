Skyborne: Three Weeks of Flight

October 17, 2026

Three weeks ago, Skyborne was a hero standing over a dusk-lit city. Today he flies.

Between Sep 23 and Oct 13 we went through six builds. The first one gave us a sunset skyline and our first HUD. Next came open terrain, then a whole planet of biomes, then a streamed New York you can land in. The latest builds add chases, rescues and flight from street level all the way up to orbit.

The sheet below has one frame from each build, so you can watch the world grow. It's still early and rough in places, and that's the fun part.
