02 / Artists

Voices of the label.

A01First artist

ValenVox

The music project of Valentin Voineag, a Michigan-based songwriter who writes his own lyrics and builds each track with AI music tools. The catalog runs from cinematic orchestral scores and Romanian folk instrumentals to worship pop, art-pop ballads and synthwave: songs about faith, longing, ascent and home.

04 / AI music

Songs you can watch.

V01

"Thirsty Like The River"

A modern worship anthem inspired by Psalm 42 — a cry from the dry places of the soul, longing for the presence of God. This song captures the tension of feeling surrounded yet alone, wrestling with doubt, and choosing praise in the middle of pain. “Deep calls to deep” becomes the heartbeat: even in the storm, hope still answers. A reminder that when the soul is thirsty, only God can satisfy.

V02

Psalms 43

A worship anthem drawn from Psalm 42 and Psalm 43—a prayer for God’s light to break through seasons of dryness, doubt, and waiting. This song carries the cry of a weary soul choosing hope again, asking to be led back to the presence of God. From the valley to the holy mountain, it’s a declaration that praise will rise even before the answer comes.

V03

Acts 2 - "Trust me this will stick in your head"

A high-energy Pentecost anthem inspired by Acts 2 and the prophecy of Joel. This song captures the sound of the upper room—the rushing wind, tongues of fire, and the bold outpouring of the Holy Spirit. It’s a declaration of unity, revival, and the promise that what God poured out then, He is still pouring out now. “This is that” becomes the cry of a generation hungry for fresh fire.
